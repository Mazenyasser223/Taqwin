# How do I log food in Taqwin?

Food logging tracks what you ate against your **daily calorie and macro targets** from your AI diet plan. Logged meals appear on the dashboard and in AI coach context so suggestions stay realistic.

## Where to log food

1. **Dashboard → Today** — each meal slot (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack) shows planned foods and a log/check control.
2. **Nutrition library** — open **Nutrition** from the nav, search Taqwin's food catalog (FoodItem + Webteb), pick a food, enter quantity, and log.
3. **AI coach** — ask "how do I log lunch?" for step-by-step help; the coach explains the UI but does not log food unless a confirmed tool action exists.

## Step-by-step: log from the dashboard

1. Open **Dashboard** and find **Today's meals** (or the diet section for the selected day).
2. Tap a **meal slot** (e.g. Breakfast).
3. If the plan lists foods, adjust portions if needed, then tap **Log meal** / the check control to record everything in that slot.
4. To add extra items, tap **Add food** on that slot — you are taken to the nutrition search, then return to the dashboard after confirming quantity.
5. Logged meals show as completed; macros and calories update on the day summary.

## Step-by-step: log from the nutrition library

1. Go to **Nutrition** (food library).
2. Search by name (Arabic or English). Results come from Taqwin's database only — not invented brands.
3. Tap a food to open details. Choose a **serving unit** (grams, piece, cup, etc.) and enter **quantity**.
4. Confirm — the food is saved to your log for today with scaled calories, protein, carbs, and fat.
5. If a food is missing from the catalog, you cannot log it until it exists in the database (custom foods may be added in future flows).

## Plan meals vs free logging

- **Plan meal logging** — marks the official slot from your 7-day diet plan as eaten (uses planned `foodItemId` / `webtebId` entries).
- **Ad-hoc logging** — add individual foods from search without a plan slot.
- You can **edit or remove** a logged entry from the meal slot before the day ends (dashboard meal editor).

## What the AI coach sees

- Your **food logs** and **today's targets** are sent to the coach when you chat.
- The coach suggests meals using the same catalog IDs — it will not invent macros for foods not in Taqwin.

## Tips

- Log consistently so dashboard charts and coach answers reflect reality.
- Use **grams** when possible for accurate macros; piece sizes use catalog serving definitions.
- Religious diet and allergies from onboarding still filter plan foods; logging does not override those rules.
