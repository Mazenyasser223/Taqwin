# Where is my weekly workout plan?

Your **workout plan** and **diet plan** are generated after onboarding and shown on the **Dashboard** — you do not need to open AI chat to see them.

## Workout plan structure

- **4 weeks** × **7 days** per week.
- Each training day lists exercises from Taqwin's exercise catalog (`exerciseId` per set).
- **Rest days** are included — the dashboard shows "Rest day" when no exercises are scheduled.

## Where to view it

1. **Dashboard → Today** — shows **today's workout** (exercises, sets, reps) and a link such as **Open workout plan**.
2. **Full workout plan view** — browse other days and weeks with **week arrows** or a day picker.
3. Tap any day to see that day's official workout and meals for the same date.

## Diet plan (7-day)

- **7 days** of meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack) with foods from the Taqwin catalog.
- Today's meals appear on the dashboard under **Today's meals** with calories and macros.
- Use week/day navigation to preview upcoming days (same calendar pattern as workouts).

## After onboarding

- Completing the questionnaire triggers **AI plan generation** when configured.
- If generation fails validation twice, Taqwin saves a **deterministic fallback plan** so the dashboard still works.
- Check plan status under **Profile → AI Plan** (regenerate, view explanation).

## Regenerating your plan

- **Profile → Regenerate plan** creates a new plan version; the previous plan is deactivated.
- Regeneration uses your latest onboarding answers (goals, injuries, allergies, budget).

## Logging against the plan

- **Workout:** open today's session on the dashboard and log sets/reps or mark completion.
- **Meals:** log each meal slot when you eat (see food logging guide).

## AI coach and the plan

- Ask the coach "what's my workout today?" — it reads your **active plan** from your profile, same as the dashboard.
- The coach does not replace the plan UI; use the dashboard for the full weekly calendar.
