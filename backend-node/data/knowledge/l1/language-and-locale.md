# How to change language in Taqwin

Taqwin supports **English** and **Arabic** (Egyptian UI copy). You can switch language without losing your plan, logs, or community account.

## Change language in Settings (recommended)

1. Open **Settings** from the profile menu or navigation.
2. Under **Preferences**, find **Language**.
3. Select **English** or **Arabic (العربية)**.
4. The choice saves to your account and updates the whole app immediately (menus, dashboard, onboarding labels).

## Quick language toggle

- A **language toggle** (EN / ع) may appear in the header on some screens — tap it to switch between English and Arabic.
- The toggle updates local display and syncs with your saved settings when you are logged in.

## AI coach language

- **Default coach replies:** Egyptian Arabic (عامية مصرية) when you write in Arabic.
- If you write in **English** or set locale to `en`, the coach replies in simple English.
- Food and exercise **names** in answers follow your UI language when both Arabic and English names exist in the catalog.

## What changes with language

| Area | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| App UI | Full translation (nav, dashboard, community, settings) |
| Food catalog labels | Arabic Webteb names or English USDA-style labels |
| Exercise library | Bilingual names and instructions where available |
| AI coach chat | Matches your message language / locale setting |
| Dates and numbers | Formatted for `ar-EG` or `en-US` locale |

## Arabic vs English content

- Platform help articles exist in **both** English and Arabic knowledge docs where noted (`locale: ar` chunks rank higher for Arabic queries).
- Coaching book excerpts (L5) are mostly English; the coach summarizes principles in your language.

## Troubleshooting

- If the app still shows the wrong language after changing Settings, refresh the page or sign out and back in.
- Language is stored in your user settings (`language: en | ar`) and in local storage for faster load.
