# Gyms and membership in Taqwin

Taqwin connects **athletes** with **partner gyms** and optional **gym memberships**. Gym owners use a separate dashboard; this guide is for athletes using the mobile/web app.

## Gyms Near Me

1. Open **Gyms Near Me** from the main navigation.
2. Browse **partner gyms** with location, amenities, and status (active, busy, quiet).
3. Tap a gym for details — photos, address, and check-in options.
4. **Check in** when you arrive at a partner gym (members-only gyms require an active membership).

## Gym membership (athlete)

- Your **dashboard profile card** shows membership status: **Free** or **Active** with renewal date when applicable.
- Active membership may unlock gym check-in, member-only locations, and Pro-style badges on your profile.
- To **join or renew**, select a partner gym and follow the in-app membership flow (or contact the gym owner if billing is handled offline).

## Freeze or cancel membership

- Open **Support** or contact the **gym owner** through a support ticket.
- Gym owners can pause membership from their **Members** list until you return.
- Taqwin support FAQ: "How do I freeze my membership?" — use Gyms Near Me → select your gym → support ticket.

## Trainers and gym owners

- **Trainers** and **coaches** may appear in Community browse; following them does not automatically assign them as your plan coach unless your gym workflow says so.
- **Gym owners** manage members, check-ins, and listings from the **Gym Dashboard** (separate login role).
- Athletes do not edit gym business profiles — only view and check in.

## Onboarding and gym selection

- During onboarding you may pick a **home gym** or workout location (home, gym, both).
- This affects exercise selection in your 4-week plan (e.g. gym equipment vs home bodyweight).

## Reward points and engagement

- **Reward points** track consistency — workouts logged, gym check-ins, and community activity.
- Points and badges are separate from paid gym membership unless your gym links them in a promotion.

## What the AI coach does not do

- The coach does **not** process payments, cancel gym contracts, or change gym owner settings.
- For billing disputes, contact the gym or Taqwin support — not the AI chat.
