# How does community work in Taqwin?

Taqwin **Community** is the social hub inside the athlete app. Open **Community** from the main navigation to post updates, watch stories, message other athletes, browse profiles, and join groups.

## Community tabs

| Tab | What you do |
|-----|-------------|
| **Feed** | Scroll posts from people you follow and recommended athletes |
| **Profile** | Your community profile, posts, and saved content |
| **Browse** | Search athletes, coaches, and gym owners |
| **Groups** | Join or create fitness groups |
| **Inbox** | Direct messages and group chats |
| **Settings** | Privacy, mentions, and notification preferences |

## Posts and feed

- Tap the composer at the top of **Feed** to write a post (text, photos, or video).
- Switch between **For you** and **Following** feed filters.
- Like, comment, and repost on posts. Pull to refresh or use the refresh button.
- When creating or editing a post you can lock comments, lock reposts, mention people or gyms, and control sharing in **Community → Settings → Privacy**.

## Stories

- The **Stories bar** appears above the feed (similar to other social apps).
- Tap your avatar with **+** to upload a photo or short video story.
- Tap another athlete's ring to watch their story full-screen.
- Stories expire after 24 hours. New stories from people you follow appear automatically.

## Inbox (direct messages)

- Open **Community → Inbox** for private chats.
- **Primary** shows accepted conversations; **Requests** shows message requests you can accept or decline.
- Start a new chat from the **+** button — search for an athlete by name.
- Send text, voice notes, and images. Create **group chats** with multiple members.
- A floating inbox shortcut may appear elsewhere in the app for quick access.

## Groups

- **Community → Groups** lists groups you belong to and groups you can discover.
- Join public groups or accept invites. Group admins can manage members and group info.

## Browse and follow

- **Browse** lets you search the Taqwin directory (athletes, coaches, gym owners).
- Open a profile to follow someone; their posts appear in your **Following** feed.

## What community is not

- Community posts are **not** medical advice. Training and diet questions belong in **AI Coach** chat or your dashboard plan.
- The AI coach does **not** read your DMs or post on your behalf unless a future tool explicitly confirms an action.
