# Help — what can the Taqwin coach do?

If you are not sure what to ask, start here. The **Smart Coach** helps athletes with training, nutrition, and using the Taqwin app.

## What you can ask about

- **Getting started** — complete onboarding, generate your first workout and diet plan, find them on the dashboard.
- **Today's plan** — meals and workout for today (same data as the dashboard).
- **Food logging** — how to log breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks from Taqwin's food catalog.
- **Workout plan** — where to find your weekly plan, rest days, and how to log sets.
- **Exercise alternatives** — swaps when you have pain or limited equipment.
- **Platform features** — community, gym membership, language settings, profile, subscription.
- **Your progress** — weight trend, logs, and targets when you ask about your own data.

## Short messages like "help"

When you send a vague message such as **help** or **I don't understand**, the coach may ask which area you need:

1. **Workout or exercise** — programs, sets, alternatives.
2. **Nutrition or meals** — macros, meal ideas from the catalog.
3. **App / platform help** — dashboard, logging, community, language.
4. **Your data today** — what you logged, your plan, your progress.

You can reply with one of those topics for a focused answer.

## How to get better answers

- Be specific: "How do I log lunch?" works better than "help".
- Write in **English** or **Arabic** — the coach mirrors your language.
- For app navigation ("where is my plan?", "change language", "export workout history"), ask directly — Taqwin internal docs answer these.
