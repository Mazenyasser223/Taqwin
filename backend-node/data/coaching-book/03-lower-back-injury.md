# Lower-Back Injury Modifications

For users with current or recent lower-back issues, avoid **deadlifts, good mornings, bent-over rows, and any exercise that loads spinal flexion under heavy weight**. Choose supported, neutral-spine alternatives:

- Replace barbell deadlift with **trap bar deadlift to a higher block** or **hip thrust**.
- Replace bent-over row with **chest-supported row** or **seated cable row**.
- Add daily **dead bug**, **bird dog**, and **side plank** to build trunk stability.
- Skip loaded carries until cleared.

Cue: brace the abdomen before every rep (think "short, hard cough") and keep the spine neutral — neither rounded nor over-arched. Stop any set if a rep produces shooting pain into the leg.
