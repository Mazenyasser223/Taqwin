# Sustainable Fat Loss

The body burns fat when it spends more energy than it eats. Engineer the deficit safely:

- **Calorie deficit:** 300-500 kcal below maintenance is the sweet spot. Anything beyond that is hard to sustain past 4-6 weeks and erodes muscle and performance.
- **Protein floor:** 1.8-2.2 g per kg of body weight to preserve lean mass during the deficit.
- **Training:** keep strength training 3-4 times per week. Cut training volume by ~10%, not strength. The goal of training in a deficit is *to keep muscle*, not to burn calories.
- **Cardio:** add 1-3 sessions of 20-40 minutes of moderate cardio (zone 2) per week. Daily walking (8-12k steps) outperforms occasional HIIT for fat loss.

Refeeds and diet breaks:

- Every 8-12 weeks of dieting, take **1-2 weeks at maintenance calories**. This restores hormones (leptin, thyroid) and willpower.
- One **carb-focused refeed day** every 7-10 days during the deficit boosts performance for the next training week.

Track weight as a 7-day average, not daily. A 0.4-0.7% drop in body weight per week is realistic; anything faster usually costs muscle.
