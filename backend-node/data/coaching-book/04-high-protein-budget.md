# High Protein on a Budget

To reach 1.6-2.2 g of protein per kg of body weight without spending too much, lean on the following inexpensive staples (per local availability in Egypt and the Gulf):

- **Eggs** — 6 g protein each, often the cheapest complete protein per gram.
- **Foul medames (fava beans)** — 8 g protein per 100 g, very filling and culturally familiar.
- **Lentils, chickpeas, white beans** — 8-9 g protein per 100 g cooked.
- **Chicken thighs (skin-off)** — typically half the price of chicken breast with similar protein.
- **Plain whole-milk yogurt or labneh** — 5-10 g protein per 100 g.
- **Whey protein isolate** — last 5-10 g of the day, when whole foods are full.

Pair every meal with a deliberate protein source and a vegetable. Cook protein in bulk twice a week (4-6 portions) to avoid skipping meals.
