# إيه هو Progressive Overload؟

**Progressive overload** (الحمل التدريجي المتزايد) = إنك **تزود التوتر على العضلات مع الوقت** عشان تجبر الجسم يadapt ويبني muscle.

## مش burn ولا pump

- **Burn (lactic acid):** إحساس — مش driver أساسي للنمو.
- **Pump:** blood in muscle — psychological boost، مش primary growth signal.
- **Driver الحقيقي:** progressive **tension** in muscle fibers — heavier weights or more reps at same weight over weeks.

## إزاي تطبّقه

1. **Add weight:** لما تكمل top of rep range (مثلاً 3×6 @ 100kg) → 102.5kg next session.
2. **Add reps:** same weight — 6,6,5 → 6,6,6 → then add weight.
3. **Track:** log sets in Taqwin — بدون tracking، مش هتعرف if you progressed.

## Rep ranges (BLS)

- **Strength/hypertrophy blend:** often **4–6 reps** heavy on compounds.
- **Accessories:** 8–12 sometimes — still progressive.

## أخطاء شائعة

- **Same weight forever** — plateau guaranteed.
- **Too much too soon** — injury؛ 2.5–5kg jumps on upper، 5–10kg lower max typical.
- **Changing exercises every week** — can't measure overload.
