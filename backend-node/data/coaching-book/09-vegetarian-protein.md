# Vegetarian and Vegan Protein

Building muscle without animal flesh is realistic. Spread protein across 3-5 meals so each meal carries 25-40 g protein from any combination below:

- **Lentils, chickpeas, kidney beans** — 8-9 g per 100 g cooked. Combine with rice or bread to cover all essential amino acids.
- **Tofu, tempeh** — 12-20 g per 100 g, fast to cook and absorb a marinade.
- **Greek yogurt, labneh, paneer, cottage cheese** — for lacto-vegetarian users only.
- **Eggs** — for ovo-vegetarian users only.
- **Whey or pea/soy protein powder** — to close the day's gap.

Pair plant proteins to maximize quality: lentils + rice, chickpeas + bread, beans + corn. Add vitamin C (lemon, peppers, tomato) to improve iron absorption from plant sources.

If the user is **strictly vegan**, ensure vitamin B12, vitamin D, omega-3 (ALA / algal DHA), and iodine come from a supplement or fortified foods. Without these, performance and recovery suffer regardless of training quality.
