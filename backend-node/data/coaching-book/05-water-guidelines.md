# Daily Water Targets

A practical target is **30-35 ml of water per kg of body weight** for a sedentary adult, plus **500-1000 ml extra per hour of intense training**. Examples:

- 70 kg sedentary: ~2.0-2.5 L/day baseline.
- 80 kg training 1 hr: ~3.0 L/day.
- 90 kg outdoors in heat: ~3.5-4.0 L/day.

Spread intake across the day. Drink **300-500 ml on waking** to rehydrate after sleep. Sip **200-300 ml every 20 min** during training sessions longer than 45 min, especially in hot climates.

Coffee, tea, watermelon, cucumber, and soup all count toward total fluid intake, but unsweetened water should make up the majority. Urine color (pale straw) is a simple daily check.
