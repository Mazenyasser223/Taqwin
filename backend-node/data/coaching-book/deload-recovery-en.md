# When to deload and how many rest days

**Deload** = one week (or 3–5 days) with reduced loads and volume so the body recovers. **Rest days** = no lifting — not laziness; part of muscle growth.

## Signs you need a deload

- Performance **flat or down** for 2–3 weeks despite sleep and nutrition.
- Persistent joint ache (not normal DOMS).
- General fatigue, irritability, or insomnia after heavy training.
- **Planned deload** every 4–8 weeks preventively — even if you feel fine.

## Practical deload

- **Volume:** Half your usual sets (e.g. 3×5 instead of 4×8).
- **Intensity:** 60–70% of recent working weight — stop short of failure.
- **Cardio:** Light or pause.
- After deload: ramp back gradually — many lifters feel stronger.

## Rest days per week

- **Beginner:** 3–4 training days, 3–4 rest days.
- **Intermediate/advanced:** 4–5 training days — **48–72 hours** between heavy sessions for same muscle group.
- **Over 40 or slow recovery:** Extra rest day between hard lower and upper.

## Active vs complete rest

- **Active recovery:** Walk, stretch, light yoga — fine.
- **Complete rest:** Sleep + adequate food — best after very hard leg day or during deload.

- Ask "when to deload?" or "how many rest days?" — coach uses L5 principles + your CAG.
- **sick** or **injury_flare** life modes need lower intensity than a standard deload.
