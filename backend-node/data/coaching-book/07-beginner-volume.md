# Volume and Frequency for Beginners

A new lifter recovers slower than they realize and adapts fastest from **moderate volume hit multiple times per week**. Useful guidelines for the first 12 weeks:

- **3-4 sessions per week**, full-body or upper/lower split.
- **8-12 working sets per muscle group per week** (e.g. chest gets 3 sets x 3 sessions = 9 sets).
- **Reps in reserve (RIR) 2-3** — never grind reps to failure on compound lifts.
- **Two rest days** scattered through the week, not back-to-back.

Compound lifts deserve more frequency than isolation. A beginner squatting twice a week with 5 working sets each session will out-progress one heavy squat day every Saturday.

Do not chase soreness — soreness is not a measure of growth. Track if reps or load go up each week; if both stall for two weeks, add one rest day or shave 1-2 sets off the week.
