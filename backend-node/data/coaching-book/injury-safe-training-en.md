# Training safely with pain or injury

Taqwin coach **does not replace a physician**. Acute pain, swelling, or numbness — see a doctor. The coach helps you **modify** training and **avoid** movements that increase pain.

## life_mode: injury_flare

- Enable **injury_flare** when pain flares — plan reduces volume and loads; CAG injury constraints apply.
- Log memory: "shoulder pain on bench press" — swap search excludes that pattern.

## General principles

- **Pain during exercise:** If pain > 3/10 or worsens with reps → **stop** that movement immediately.
- **Catalog swaps:** Ask "bench alternative with shoulder pain" — coach returns safe L2 exercises + L5 principles.
- **Volume:** Cut sets 30–50% for affected area; maintain other body parts if medically cleared.

## Common patterns (general info — not diagnosis)

### Shoulder

- Avoid **overhead press** and **deep dips** with anterior shoulder pain.
- Swaps: neutral-grip press, landmine press, light lateral raises — valid exerciseId from library.

### Knee

- Avoid **deep squat** and heavy **leg extension** with patellar pain.
- Swaps: limited-range leg press, RDL, hip thrust — per medical guidance.

### Lower back

- Avoid **heavy deadlift** and **good mornings** during flare-up.
- Swaps: machine row, chest-supported row, walking — light core stability.

## Recovery

- **Deload week** when pain subsides — not necessarily complete rest unless doctor orders.
- Return loads gradually (max ~10% weekly increase on affected pattern).
- Update **medical notes** in profile — coach and plan validator read constraints.
