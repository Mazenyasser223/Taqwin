# فلسفة التمرين في BLS (ملخص)

ملخص عربي لمبادئ **Training Philosophy** — program design، split، و focus على compound lifts.

## Core philosophy

- **Compound lifts first:** squat، bench، deadlift، overhead press، rows — bulk of progress.
- **Progressive overload** on these lifts — track weights in Taqwin workout log.
- **Volume landmarks:** enough sets per muscle per week — not endless junk volume.
- **Intensity:** heavy relative to rep range — stop 1–2 reps before failure on main lifts (BLS style).

## Split و frequency

- **Push/pull/legs** or **upper/lower** common in BLS — 4–5 days typical.
- **Same muscle** not trained heavy daily — respect 48–72h+ recovery.
- **Accessories** support weak points — not replace compounds.

## What to avoid

- **Bro split** every day single muscle if recovery poor.
- **Excessive techniques:** drop sets، supersets marathon as primary driver.
- **Ego lifting** with bad form — injury > short-term PR.
