# Training With Knee Pain

When a user reports knee pain or a recent knee injury, **never prescribe deep squats, pistol squats, jumping, sprinting, or box jumps** without explicit clearance. Use safer substitutions and keep the joint loaded in a strong position:

- Replace barbell back squat with **goblet squat to a box** or **leg press** (limited range).
- Replace lunges and Bulgarian split squats with **glute bridges** and **hip thrusts**.
- Replace jumping with **brisk walking**, **stationary bike**, or **rowing**.
- Avoid loaded knee flexion past 90° if pain is reported below the kneecap.

Train the muscles that protect the knee: glutes, hamstrings, quads, and calves through pain-free ranges. Stop any rep that causes sharp pain.

If the user has not been cleared by a doctor and the pain is acute, suggest a rest day and rest plus gentle mobility (heel slides, terminal knee extensions).
