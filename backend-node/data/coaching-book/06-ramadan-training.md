# Training and Nutrition During Ramadan

When a user is fasting from sunrise to sunset, schedule training **30-60 minutes before iftar** (so the post-workout meal arrives fast) or **2-3 hours after iftar** (when carbs are available for performance).

Nutrition principles:

- **Suhoor (pre-dawn):** slow-release carbs (oats, foul medames, whole-grain bread), 30-40 g of protein (eggs, labneh, leftover chicken), 1-2 cups of water. Avoid heavy salty foods that increase daytime thirst.
- **Iftar:** start with dates and water, then a balanced meal — lean protein, fibrous vegetables, a smart starch (rice, freekeh, sweet potato). Do not over-eat.
- **Between iftar and suhoor:** sip 2-3 L of water total. A protein shake or yogurt before bed protects muscle.

Reduce training volume by **20-30%** versus normal weeks, prioritize technique, and accept that this is a maintenance phase. Strength typically returns within 2 weeks after Eid with full meals and sleep restored.
