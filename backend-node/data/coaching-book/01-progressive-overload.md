# Progressive Overload

Progressive overload is the gradual increase of training stress so the body keeps adapting. Without it, training plateaus. Apply it through any of these levers — change one at a time:

- **Load** — add 2.5-5 kg when all prescribed reps are completed with two reps in reserve.
- **Reps** — add 1-2 reps at the same load before raising weight.
- **Sets** — add 1 working set per week, capped at a sensible total weekly volume per muscle (10-20 sets for most lifters).
- **Tempo / Range of Motion** — slower eccentrics (3-4 seconds down) or fuller range increase tension without more load.

Track every working set. If progress stalls for two weeks, reduce volume by ~20% for one week (a deload) and resume.

For beginners, focus on adding reps and load on compound lifts. For intermediate lifters, manipulate volume and exercise variation.
