# Training while traveling

Travel disrupts routine — the goal is **consistency**, not personal records. Taqwin suggests catalog exercises or bodyweight options that fit hotel equipment.

## Before you leave

- Enable **life mode: travel** in coach — weekly plan simplifies (often 3× bodyweight or light dumbbells).
- Prepare **swap list** for main lifts (bench → push-ups, squat → goblet or lunges).
- If the hotel has a gym, ask coach for "today's workout" with available equipment.

## Sample travel session (30–45 min)

1. **Warm-up:** 5 min mobility + walk or bands.
2. **Push:** Push-ups, dumbbell press, chair dips.
3. **Pull:** Inverted rows, band rows, or lat pulldown if available.
4. **Legs:** Goblet squat, lunges, leg press if machine exists.
5. **Core:** Plank 3×30–60 seconds.

## Nutrition on the road

- **Protein** every meal — restaurants, bars, yogurt.
- Log approximate calories in Taqwin FoodItem — avoid "vacation mode" drift.
- **Hydration:** Travel dehydrates — drink more than usual.

## No gym available

- **Bodyweight circuit** 3 rounds: 10 push-ups, 15 squats, 10 band rows, 30s plank.
- **Stairs or brisk walk** 20 minutes counts as activity.
- Ramp intensity back after return — one transition week before max loads.

## When to rest

- Severe jet lag or under 5 hours sleep → **rest or light yoga** instead of heavy legs.
- Ask coach to adapt the plan: "adjust workout for travel".
